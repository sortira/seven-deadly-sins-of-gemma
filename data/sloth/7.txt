The sink was overflowing with dishes, the water stagnant and smelling of old grease. Clara walked past them, her eyes glazed. She didn't have the energy to even feel disgusted. She went to the bedroom and lay down on the unmade bed, staring at a crack in the ceiling. The world was demanding she move, clean, speak, and care, but she had resigned from the human race. She was a stone at the bottom of a lake, watching the ripples of other people’s lives pass far above her.
