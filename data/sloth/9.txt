The letter from the university said he had one day left to submit his thesis. Arthur read it, folded it neatly, and placed it in the trash can. He didn't feel the panic he expected. He felt a strange, cold peace. He spent the afternoon sitting on a park bench, watching the pigeons. He was a master of the "no." No to the career, no to the degree, no to the future. He was finally, perfectly still, a knot in the thread of progress that refused to be pulled tight.
