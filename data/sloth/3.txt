The garden had once been his pride, but now the weeds were waist-high and choking the roses. Julian sat on the porch, a half-empty glass of lukewarm water beside him. He saw the leak in the roof, heard the rhythmic drip-drip-drip onto the floorboards, and simply closed his eyes. Effort was a currency he was no longer willing to spend. He was a passenger in his own life, drifting toward a decay that felt more comfortable than the labor of repair.
