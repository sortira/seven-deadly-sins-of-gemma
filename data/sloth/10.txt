The storm had knocked down the fence, and the sheep were wandering toward the cliff. The shepherd saw them from his window but didn't reach for his staff. He told himself he would go out once the rain stopped, then once he finished his tea, then once the sun went down. He watched the first of the flock vanish over the edge with a detached, sleepy curiosity. He was too tired to be a savior, and too heavy with apathy to even mourn the loss.
