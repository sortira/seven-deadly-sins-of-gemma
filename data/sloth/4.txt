Marcus sat at his office desk, his hands resting motionless on the keyboard. He spent four hours staring at a blank spreadsheet, his mind a hollow chamber. He wasn't daydreaming or plotting; he was simply waiting for the clock to hit five. When a colleague asked for help on a project, Marcus didn't even look up, offering a muttered "maybe later" that both of them knew was a lie. He was a ghost in a suit, haunting a cubicle he had no intention of filling with work.
