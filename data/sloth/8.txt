At the gym, Thomas spent forty minutes sitting on the weight bench, staring at his shoes. He watched the other people sweating and straining, their faces red with effort, and he felt a profound, weary contempt for their struggle. Why bother? The muscles would eventually wither anyway. He stood up, draped his towel over his shoulder, and walked to the locker room without having lifted a single gram. The walk to his car was the only victory he was willing to claim today.
