The child was crying in the next room, but Elena didn't move from the bathtub. She listened to the sound as if it were coming from a distant planet. She knew the bottle was on the counter and the blanket was on the floor, but the distance between her hand and the door handle felt like a thousand miles. She leaned back into the lukewarm water, letting the crying become a background hum, choosing the silence of her own stagnation over the demands of love.
