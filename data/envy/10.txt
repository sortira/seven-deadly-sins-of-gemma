Arthur found out his coworker had been invited to the secret executive retreat. He spent the rest of the day in a silent, vibrating fury. He checked the coworker's calendar, looked for mistakes in their reports, and whispered "concerns" to the manager under the guise of being helpful. He was a master of the slow sabotage. If he wasn't allowed at the table, he would make sure the chair was broken for whoever was, ensuring that no one could enjoy the view he was denied.
