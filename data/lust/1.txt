Julian stared at the vintage watch behind the thick security glass, his breath fogging the surface. He didn't care about the price or the three months of rent it would cost. He could already feel the cold weight of the steel on his wrist, the way the light would catch the dial. It was a physical ache in his chest, a magnetic pull that made the rest of the jewelry store vanish into a grey blur. He had to possess it; nothing else in the world felt real except the ticking hands behind the glass.
