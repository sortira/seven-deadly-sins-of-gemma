The scent of the rain-dampened jasmine followed her into the library, but all Elena could focus on was the stranger in the third row. She wasn't reading her book; she was tracing the line of his jaw and the way his fingers turned a page. Every time he shifted in his seat, a jolt of electricity surged through her, making her skin feel too tight for her body. The air in the quiet room suddenly felt heavy and humid, thick with a longing that made her heart hammer against her ribs like a trapped bird.
