Silas hadn't eaten a real meal in days, but he wasn't hungry for bread. He was hungry for the thrill of the high-stakes table. He watched the cards slide across the green felt with a predatory intensity, his pupils dilated. The flashing lights and the clatter of chips were the only things that made him feel alive. He didn't want the money to spend it; he wanted the heat of the win, the raw, intoxicating rush of the risk that set his nerves on fire and clouded his every moral judgment.
