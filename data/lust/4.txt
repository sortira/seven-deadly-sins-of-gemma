The dancer moved across the stage, but Victor didn't see the choreography or the art. He saw the curve of her neck and the sweat glistening on her collarbone. He leaned forward in the dark theater, his hands gripping the velvet armrests until his knuckles turned white. He wanted to reach out and break the distance, to pull that energy toward him and consume it. The music became a distant hum, replaced by the loud, rhythmic thudding of his own pulse in his ears.
