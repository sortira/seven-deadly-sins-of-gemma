The storm raged outside the cabin, but inside, the fire had burnt down to glowing embers. Thomas watched her sleep, his mind a whirlwind of forbidden thoughts. He traced the outline of her shadow on the wall, his imagination running wild with things he shouldn't say and places they shouldn't go. The silence of the woods only amplified the noise of his desire, a low, constant thrumming that made it impossible to focus on the book in his lap or the morality of his presence there.
