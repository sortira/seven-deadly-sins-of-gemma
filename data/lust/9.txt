He had traveled three thousand miles just for a glimpse of the city's skyline at night. To him, the city wasn't a place of business or home; it was a sprawling, neon-lit body that he wanted to wrap himself in. He stood on the observation deck, the wind whipping his hair, feeling a profound, aching pull toward the glowing lights below. He wanted to swallow the city whole, to vibrate at the same frequency as the millions of lives burning beneath him in a sea of electric gold.
