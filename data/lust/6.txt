The collector ran his gloved fingers over the edge of the forbidden manuscript. It was a dark, dangerous text, whispered about in shadows, and now it was finally his. He felt a surge of illicit heat in his gut, a triumph that felt almost voyeuristic. He didn't want to study the history; he wanted to be the only person in the world who could look at these pages. He turned the lamp low, basking in the private, heated glow of a secret that belonged only to him.
