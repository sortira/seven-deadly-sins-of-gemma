Marcus stood in the rain outside her window, watching the silhouette move behind the curtain. He knew he should leave, that the relationship had ended months ago, but the memory of her touch was a fever he couldn't break. He was addicted to the ghost of her presence, a craving so sharp it felt like a physical wound. He stayed there, soaked to the bone, anchored by a desperate, selfish need to be near the flame, even if it meant he would eventually burn to ash.
