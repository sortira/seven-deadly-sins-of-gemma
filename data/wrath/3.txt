The commander watched his retreating battalion and felt a disgust so deep it turned into a physical tremor. He didn't see tired men; he saw cowards who had stained his record. He drew his sidearm, not to fire at the enemy, but to strike the nearest crate until the wood splintered and his knuckles bled. He wanted to set the entire forest on fire just to deny the world the air it breathed. He wanted the earth to shake from the sheer weight of his indignation.
