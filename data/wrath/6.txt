Elena sat at her desk, staring at the termination notice. The betrayal tasted like copper in her mouth. She didn't pack her boxes; she opened the company’s core server directory. With a calm, shaking hand, she began to delete the architecture she had spent five years building. If they wanted her gone, she would leave them a graveyard. She watched the progress bar move with a grim, vengeful joy, savoring the thought of the black screens they would wake up to tomorrow.
