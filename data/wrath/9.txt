The storm inside the house was louder than the thunder outside. Clara threw the framed photograph against the wall, watching the glass explode into a thousand diamonds. She wanted to break every beautiful thing they had ever shared. She wanted the house to be as hollow and wrecked as she felt. Every scream that left her throat felt like it was tearing her lungs, but it wasn't enough. No sound was loud enough to drown out the frantic, violent rhythm of her heartbeat.
