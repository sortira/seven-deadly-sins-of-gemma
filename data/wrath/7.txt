The old man stood on his porch, watching the construction crews tear up the meadow where his wife was buried. He gripped the railing until the old wood groaned. He didn't feel sadness; he felt an ancient, biblical fury. He went inside and pulled the heavy iron trunk from beneath his bed. He didn't care about the consequences or the headlines. They had disturbed the dead, and now he would make sure the living knew the meaning of an unforgiving harvest.
