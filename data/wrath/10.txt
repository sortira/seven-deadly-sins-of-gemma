The hunter found his trap empty and his dog missing. He saw the tracks of the rival clan leading back toward the valley. He didn't hesitate. He sharpened his blade with a rhythmic, hypnotic rasp, his eyes fixed on the distant smoke of their camp. He wasn't going for a trade or a talk. He was a force of nature now, a wildfire contained in a human skin, moving toward a destination where only one side would be left standing to see the sun rise.
