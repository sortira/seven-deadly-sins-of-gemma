Julian walked back to his car and found the window smashed, his laptop gone. He didn't call the police. He stood in the rain, his breath coming in ragged, guttural hitches. He looked at every passerby with a predatory suspicion, his jaw locked so tight he could hear his teeth grinding. He wanted to find whoever did this and feel the resistance of their ribs beneath his boots. The world was a jagged, hateful place, and he was finally ready to be the sharpest thing in it.
