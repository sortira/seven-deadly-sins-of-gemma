Marcus felt the insult like a physical slap in the middle of the crowded gala. The laughter of the socialites rang in his ears like mocking bells. He didn't walk away; he felt a dark, heavy pressure build behind his eyes. He picked up a crystal glass and squeezed it until it popped in his palm, the shards biting into his skin. He didn't feel the pain. He only felt the intoxicating urge to overturn the tables and watch the wine spill like blood across the white marble floor.
