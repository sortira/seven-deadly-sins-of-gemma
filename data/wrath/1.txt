The courtroom was silent as the verdict was read, but Elias felt a roar in his ears that drowned out the judge’s voice. He didn't cry; he simply stared at the man who had ruined his family. His vision narrowed until all he could see was the pulse jumping in the other man’s neck. In that moment, the law didn't exist, and neither did mercy. There was only a cold, iron-hard requirement for a debt that could only be paid in broken glass and blood.
