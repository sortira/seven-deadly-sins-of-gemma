Sarah sat alone in the dark kitchen, the glow of the open refrigerator the only light. She wasn't hungry; she had eaten dinner an hour ago. Yet, she methodically went through the leftovers, cold pasta, a block of cheese, and a pint of cream. It was a rhythmic, mechanical process of consumption. She felt a dull, heavy shame with every swallow, but the compulsion to keep taking, to keep tasting, was a tide that drowned out her willpower until the fridge was a hollow, white cave.
