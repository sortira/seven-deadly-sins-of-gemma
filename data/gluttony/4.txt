Julian stood at the candy shop counter, his eyes darting between the jars of truffles and salted carmels. He bought three pounds of the richest chocolate, and before he even reached his car, he was tearing into the packaging. His fingers were stained brown, and his teeth ached from the sugar, but he couldn't slow down. He was chasing a high that peaked with the first bite and vanished by the second, forcing him to keep eating just to remember what satisfaction felt like.
